# EVORIGEN Brand Identity

EVORIGEN is a legacy intelligence system for preserving practical judgment, expert memory, and institutional know-how. The visual identity should feel archival, precise, and calm rather than futuristic or decorative.

## Brand Position

- Promise: Preserve hard-earned knowledge as usable operational intelligence.
- Personality: Serious, custodial, intelligent, understated.
- Avoid: Neon AI styling, gradient effects, exaggerated glow, generic SaaS hero sections, overly rounded cards.

## Visual System

- Core palette: Ink `#0B0B09`, carbon `#11110E`, parchment `#F4E9D0`, stone `#A8A29E`.
- Accent palette: Aged brass `#B89B5E`, archive green `#6A8E84`, oxide red `#8E4C42`.
- Surfaces: Flat dark panels with fine borders and soft black shadows.
- Corners: 8px for cards and primary controls, 4px for badges and small tokens.
- Typography: Space Grotesk for display headings, Inter for UI, JetBrains Mono for metadata.

## UI Rules

- No gradients.
- Use restrained color to indicate priority, provenance, or status.
- Keep dashboards dense and scannable.
- Prefer labels, tables, timelines, and evidence blocks over marketing cards.
- Buttons should look functional, not promotional.
